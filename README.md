### 👨‍💻 About me

I'm an Economist working at the intersection of **sustainability**, **territorial development**, and **applied research**.

Data Science and web development are tools I use to tackle real-world economic problems, from analysing socioeconomic data to building systems that support policy and decision-making. I'm also interested in how **distributed ledger technologies** can reshape transparency and accountability in economic systems.

## 🏔️ Latest project · Chisone & Germanasca valleys

<table>
<tr>
<td>

### [The foundational economy of two Alpine valleys](https://varavallo.github.io/valli-chisone-germanasca/) <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/NEW-2026-2E7D32?style=flat-square" alt="New"/>

A bilingual one-page field study of the **14 municipalities** of the Unione Montana Valli Chisone e Germanasca, west of Turin: how the population is changing, and which everyday services — groceries, transport, health, schools, civic infrastructure, meeting places — residents still have within reach.

It applies the method of my [*Socio-Economic Planning Sciences* paper (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.seps.2026.102549) to a single territory, combining **Google Places** service counts with **ISTAT** demographic data.

| 👥 Residents (2024) | 📉 Change 2014–24 | 🎂 Mean age | 📍 Service points mapped | 🏫 Municipalities with no school |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **16,611** | **−7.6%** | **50.8** | **702** | **4 of 14** |

**Built with:** Python · GeoPandas · hand-written interactive SVG maps (every municipality is a linked `<path>`: select one and it lights up across all maps and charts) · plain HTML/CSS/JS, no framework · GitHub Pages
